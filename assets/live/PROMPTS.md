# Native live-scene assets and prompts

Generated using the built-in imagegen tool. The launcher uses these two transparent layers in a continuous native scene, not a wallpaper slideshow or video. Camera drift, wind, clouds, stars and precipitation are rendered in code. Asset paths: city.png and cloud.png in this directory.

## city

Use case: panoramic launcher backdrop for Abu Dhabi. Asset: wide 3:1 photorealistic aerial panorama of central Abu Dhabi viewed from above the Corniche shoreline. Blue Arabian Gulf curves through the lower third, with dense authentic Abu Dhabi high-rises, Etihad Towers and Landmark Tower in the middle distance, Emirates Palace gardens and waterfront buildings. Bright clear late-morning light; realistic atmospheric haze, natural stone and glass, balanced photographic color. Keep skyline legible in a wide view and extend scenery naturally to both edges for a gentle horizontal camera pan. No Dubai landmarks, dunes, text, logos, impossible architecture, extra sun disc or oversized clouds. Generated with the built-in image-generation tool.

## city-night

Use case: night state blended against the daytime Abu Dhabi panorama. Preserve the exact scene, structures, skyline placement, horizon, shoreline and camera. Clear blue-hour/night, deep indigo sky, warm illuminated windows and waterfront lights, dark Gulf reflections. No moon or sun (the weather widget owns both icons), no clouds or stars. Match the day image geometry for smooth native cross-fading. Generated by editing `city.png` with the built-in image-generation tool.

## cloud

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset: one isolated soft white cumulus/cirrus cloud bank with a genuinely transparent background, for animation over a city sky. Wide horizontal composition. Wispy edges, delicate three-dimensional volume, natural varied opacity and soft sunlit light-grey undersides. One cloud formation only, centered with generous transparent padding. No horizon, no sky color, no land, no text, no frame.
