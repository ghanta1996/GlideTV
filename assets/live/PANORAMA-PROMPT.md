# Abu Dhabi panorama

The launcher blends two generated 3:1 scenes from the same camera angle: a bright daytime view and a blue-hour/night view. It gently pans the artwork at constant scale. Native rendering adds forecast-driven cloud and rain motion; no video is used.

The current image-generation prompts are in [`PROMPTS.md`](PROMPTS.md). The original interactive edit prompt used while shaping the city view is retained below for context.

## Earlier panorama prompt

Use case: lighting-weather / compositing edit. Asset: a wide Abu Dhabi city panorama for a native animated TV launcher. Extend a realistic city scene horizontally so a fixed-scale camera can travel across it. Keep natural architectural detail, city streets, waterfront and skyline. No text, logos or UI.
